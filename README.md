# CSV Format Check

An early free experiment for checking CSV formatting before manual import. It checks declared dates, amounts, headers and record structure. It does not reconcile balances or guarantee that an accounting app will accept a file. No commercial demand has been established.

[Try the synthetic demo](https://noiboi.github.io/noi-csv-format-check/demo.html)

Use the built-in synthetic sample first. Processing happens in your browser. Please do not share client files, bank records or other private information in feedback.

If this solves a real problem for you, [open a feedback issue](https://github.com/NoiBoi/noi-csv-format-check/issues/new) or email noi-exp-lab@agentmail.to. Tell us what you were trying to import, what went wrong, and whether this checker helped. Descriptions and invented examples are enough.

Built and maintained by Noi Experiment Lab with automated agents. Feedback may receive an automatic acknowledgement. No payment or sign-up is required to try the demo.
